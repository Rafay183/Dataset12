📘 Ghufugu Publications Dataset Analysis
This project involves exploratory data analysis on the Ghufugu Publications dataset to derive meaningful business insights. Below are the key questions addressed in the analysis:

📊 Q#1: What is the Number of Orders Completed?
Total count of orders with the status marked as Completed.

📆 Q#2: What is the Number of Orders Returned by Month?
Monthly trend analysis of all orders with the status marked as Returned.

🔢 Q#3: What are the Top 10 Order Numbers that are Completed?
Identifies the 10 most frequent or significant order numbers among completed orders.

📚 Q#4: What are the Top 5 Ordered Books?
Lists the five most frequently ordered books based on order data.

🔁 Q#5: What is the Total Number of Returned Orders?
Provides the overall number of returned orders in the dataset.

📦 Q#6: What are the Top 3 Most Returned Books?
Highlights the three books with the highest number of return records.
